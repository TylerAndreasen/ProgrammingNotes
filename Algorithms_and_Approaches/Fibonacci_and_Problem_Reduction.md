# Fib

The Fibonacci Sequence contains numbers which are the sum of the two previous elements in the sequence, beginning: 1, 1.

1) Calculating the ith fibonacci value using simple recursion will take exponential time.

2) You can also create a list of memorized values and calculate the sum of the last two elements.

3) You can also just keep track of the last two values, and cycle the values through three variables.
